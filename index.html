How I Ranked My Top 10 Games

These are the criteria I used to rank my top 10 games for 2026:

  • Overall quality and polish

Note: This list is subjective and based on my personal preferences and experiences. These are the games I can't stop playing and keep coming back to,a couple I still haven't beaten yet and I hope you find some new favorites in my list!
